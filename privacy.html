Privacy Policy

AFL Performance

Last updated: 25 May 2026

Overview

AFL Performance provides team, player, PAV, comparison, and news analytics. This policy explains the information used to run the app and improve the experience.

Account Information

The app uses Supabase for account registration, login, email verification, and password reset. Supabase stores the email address you use to create an account. Passwords are handled by Supabase authentication services and are not visible inside the app.

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics to understand usage patterns, such as page views and basic product events. The app does not intentionally send passwords, email addresses, or the full text of questions to Google Analytics.

Sports Data And News

AFL Performance displays generated sports statistics and curated news links. Opening a news story may take you to the publisher's website, where that publisher's own privacy policy applies.

Data Choices

You can stop using the app at any time. To request account deletion or ask a privacy question, contact the app operator using the support page.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, visit AFL Performance Support.
